# scope

The repository is empty of content and that is its current state, not an
oversight. What it is reserved for is the portfolio itself: the work worth
showing, in whatever form it is shown.

## what does not belong here

The personal sites already exist and are not moving. `website/souhailmrn/` is
the profile site, `website/souhail/` is the consulting offer, and
`website/blog/` is the writing. A portfolio that duplicated any of them would be
a second copy going out of date the moment the first changed — link to them
instead.

## what to add first

Whatever the portfolio is *made of* — the pieces themselves and the record of
what each one was. The shape follows from that, and this document is rewritten
to describe it once it exists.
